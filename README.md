# Staff/Principal Engineer Roadmap

A comprehensive, self-paced roadmap for senior engineers transitioning to Staff/Principal roles at Tier 1 companies.

## Overview

This roadmap provides a structured path covering:

- **Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)** - Foundation for all computer science
- **Distributed Systems & Consensus** - Understanding systems at scale
- **System Design at Scale** - Architectural thinking and trade-offs
- **Technology Depth** - Expert knowledge in your core tech stack
- **Modern Software Engineering Practices** - TDD, BDD, concurrency, event-driven, observability
- **Communication, Influence & Leadership** - Making your expertise visible and influential

## Structure

The roadmap is organized into 5 stages:

1. **Stage 1: Foundations & Modern Practices** - DSA, testing, concurrency, EDA, observability
2. **Stage 2: Deep Distributed Systems & Advanced Algorithms** - Consensus, advanced data structures, replication
3. **Stage 3: System Design & Scale** - Design framework, scalability patterns, reliability, cost optimization
4. **Stage 4: Technology Depth & Modern Practices** - Deep dive into your tech stack, performance, testing
5. **Stage 5: Communication, Influence & Leadership** - Writing, speaking, mentorship, organizational navigation

## Key Features

- **Self-paced** - No fixed timeline; adapt to your circumstances
- **Comprehensive** - Covers all areas needed for staff-level competence
- **Practical** - Includes concrete projects and deliverables
- **Adaptable** - Guidance for different tech stacks and learning styles
- **Shareable** - Template for helping other senior engineers

## Getting Started

1. Read the [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) file
2. Assess your current strengths and gaps
3. Adapt the roadmap to your situation
4. Choose a starting stage
5. Create a schedule and find accountability
6. Start with Stage 1

## What You'll Accomplish

By completing this roadmap, you'll have:

- ✅ Deep algorithmic and systems thinking
- ✅ Mastery of distributed systems
- ✅ Strong system design capabilities
- ✅ Expert-level tech stack knowledge
- ✅ Published technical writing (5+ blog posts)
- ✅ Public speaking experience
- ✅ Mentored engineers
- ✅ Led org-scale technical initiatives
- ✅ Ready for Staff/Principal interviews
